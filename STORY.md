The first memory is the taste of salt.

One moment you were a scrawny **ten-year-old** queuing for soft-serve on the *Aegean Star* family-cruise; the next, a midnight cyclone tore the ship in half. When the world stopped spinning you lay face-down on sugar-white sand, ribs aching, the surf licking your sneakers. No grown-ups, no friends—just you, a cracked suitcase, and an empty shoe.

---

## Day 1 — Sand, Sun … and Fear

Instinct trumped panic:

* **Water.** A dented catering saucepan became a solar still.
* **Shelter.** Palm-leaf lean-to against a basalt overhang.
* **Signal.** A smoky *HELP* pyre on the headland—soot-black by day, ember beacon by night.

The sunset painted the sky blood-orange. Horizon Isle looked like paradise, but the howls that rose after dark proved it had teeth.

---

## Day 2 — The Feathered Turn

Scouring the tideline at dawn, you jumped at a startled **bawk!** Out waddled a caramel-feathered chicken, leg band stamped **“CS-04.”** Your empty stomach hissed *dinner*; your lonely heart whispered *friend*. You offered a damp biscuit. She pecked twice and followed at your heel.

You named her **Compass** after noticing she always drifted toward something useful—a freshwater seep, berry patch, or crab burrow—as if her beak had its own north.

---

## Weeks 1-2 — Apprentice & Master

You became the island’s youngest tinkerer, and Compass its brightest apprentice.

| Skill               | How You Taught It                        | Pay-off                                |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Forage-Scratch**  | Hid rice grains beneath edible plants    | Compass highlights herbs & roots       |
| **Perimeter Alarm** | Rewarded every warning squawk with mango | Night raids trigger early alert        |
| **Carry-Cord**      | Twine harness from shoelaces             | Ferries screws & tinder up palm trunks |
| **Egg-Cache**       | Driftwood nest box under the lean-to     | One egg every 3 days—food *and* morale |
| **Molt-Gather**     | Gentle brushing during molt              | Feathers for fletching darts & padding |

By storm-night four she even nudged glowing coals back under your tarp, saving you from hypothermia. You no longer spoke *to* the chicken; you spoke *with* her.

---

## When Paradise Bites Back

*Rock-razor crabs* gashed your ankle—Compass’s feathers padded the splint.
*Night-screech gulls* dive-bombed your fish racks—Compass flapped and shrieked them away.
A cyclone remnant ripped your roof free—you rebuilt, lashing everything down *twice*.

You salvaged treasure from wreck-litter: a solar-panel shard trickle-charged a phone battery; lifeboat ribs became a collapsible fishing gig; VR-headset optics morphed into a spyglass. Rumours spread beyond the reef about **“the kid who builds shiny things…and his compass-beaked sidekick.”**

---

## Day 19 — First Footprints

A narrow skiff nosed into the lagoon. Three charcoal-smeared raiders offered coconuts for your spear-gig. You refused. That night Compass’s alarm cries yanked you from sleep; burning drift-oil and raw courage drove them off. At dawn a sigil—**⌖**—was scorched into your shelter post: the mark of the **Tidecutters**.

## Chickens, Crafting & the Future

### Feathers

Collected during Compass’s molts; fletch blow-darts, pad jackets, craft lure flies.

### Eggs

Protein for a growing ten-year-old; powdered shells reinforce pottery glaze.

### Incubation Quest

*Blueprint unlock:* **Drift-glass Incubator**
*Requires:*

1. Solar panel shard (heat)
2. Crushed volcanic sand (thermal mass)
3. Feathers (insulation)
4. Timer spring from cruise-ship toy (turning mechanism)

Success hatches 1-3 chicks every 28 days. Train them with the same mini-games you used on Compass—only faster, thanks to her example.

A whisper among factions rises: “The boy’s island is turning into a coop of miracles.”

---

## Choices that Shape a Survivor

Protecting Compass—and her future brood—sits beside guarding water and fire:

* **Parley** with Tidecutters: trade a chick for peace? Risk losing leverage.
* **Join** Coral Kin: gain herbal cures but bind yourself to their rites.
* **Trade** a transmitter to Foundry: maybe call rescue—or invite conquest.

---

## Storm Season & Beyond

Oct-Jan tempests loom; you have three goals:

1. **Survive the storms**—reinforce camp, cache tinder in sealed crates.
2. **Decide**: fortify Horizon or build a blue-water raft.
3. **Protect Compass, her eggs, and the photo of home** tucked in your pocket—because together they remind you why you fight to stay *human*.

Some nights you lie beneath a patched tarp, Compass tucked under your arm, chicks cheeping in a bamboo crate. Distant drums echo across black water. You’re still the kid who once dreamed of arcade tickets and Saturday cartoons, yet calluses toughen your palms and a flint knife feels natural at your belt. Wonder and danger are Siamese twins.

When the next sail appears—sigil unknown—you don’t clutch Compass in fear. You whistle; she hops to your shoulder, feathers ruffling like a banner. Whatever steps onto your beach will meet a ten-year-old survivor, his bright-beaked compass, and perhaps a brood of future navigators—ready to write the next chapter of the **Isles of the Broken Compass**.
